Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book same-day appointments?

Yes, we keep a limited number of slots open for same-day appointments and emergencies. Please call our clinic directly at (650) 123-4567 to check same-day availability.

What if I need to cancel my appointment?

You can cancel your appointment up to 24 hours in advance without any charge. For cancellations with less than 24 hours notice, a $25 fee may apply. You can cancel through our online portal or by calling our clinic.

How do I prepare my pet for a checkup?

For routine checkups, bring any medical records you have, a list of medications your pet is taking, and a stool sample if possible. Keep your pet on a leash or in a carrier for safety. If your pet is anxious, let us know in advance.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards, and pet insurance. We also offer payment plans through CareCredit for larger procedures. Payment is due at the time of service.

Our Story

How we became the trusted veterinary clinic in our community

From a Small Practice to a Community Cornerstone

Founded in 2015 by Dr. Sarah Njoroge, Foothill Vethouse Clinic began as a small practice with a big vision: to provide exceptional veterinary care that prioritizes the well-being of every pet and the peace of mind of every owner.

What started as a one-veterinarian practice has grown into a full-service clinic with a team of dedicated professionals, but our core mission remains unchanged. We believe that every pet deserves compassionate, comprehensive care delivered with expertise and empathy.

Over the years, we've expanded our services, invested in advanced technology, and built lasting relationships with thousands of pet families in our community. Our commitment to excellence has made us the go-to veterinary clinic for pet owners who want the best for their furry family members.
